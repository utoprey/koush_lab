Welcome to Koush Lab

Our laboratory is part of the Vladimir Zelman Center for Neurobiology and Brain Rehabilitation at the Skolkovo Institute of Science and Technology (Skoltech).

We study brain function in health and disease using functional neuroimaging, EEG, and computational methods. Our research connects brain network dynamics and cognitive neuroscience with machine learning and real-time neurofeedback.

The laboratory is led by Yury Koush, Associate Professor.

Team membersPublicationsJoin our lab

Research Areas

  • Multimodal AI. EEG–fMRI integration, cross-modal prediction, deep learning, and foundation models.
  • Real-time neurofeedback. Real-time fMRI processing, closed-loop experiments, and open-source tools for neurofeedback research.
  • Cognitive neuroscience. Emotion regulation, social perception, and functional and effective brain connectivity.
