Latest News

Feb 2026

New paper in Stroke

Yury Koush co-authored a feasibility RCT of real-time fMRI neurofeedback for reading rehabilitation in aphasia (Boukrina et al.), targeting left supramarginal gyrus activation in left-hemisphere stroke survivors. Read paper →

Oct 2025

MICCAI 2025 Conference

Our lab presented two papers: pyOpenNFT — an open-source Python framework for ML-based real-time fMRI and EEG-fMRI neurofeedback, and CSTNet — a generative framework for EEG-to-ECoG mapping via optimal transport.

Sep 2025

New paper in PNAS

Yury Koush co-authored "Bridging feeling and motion: Insula–premotor dynamics in the processing of action vitality forms" with G. Di Cesare, P. Zeidman, A. Sciutti, K. Friston, and G. Rizzolatti — DCM analysis revealing how affective insula signals modulate the premotor cortex during action vitality. Read paper →
