Our research

From brain signals to insight.

We connect computational methods with cognitive neuroscience to study brain function in health and disease.

01 / DECODE

Multimodal AI

Bringing EEG and fMRI together through machine learning. We investigate cross-modal prediction and foundation models to connect the temporal and spatial views of brain activity.

EEG–fMRIDeep learningGenerative AI
Explore related work
02 / MODULATE

Real-time neurofeedback

Developing tools to measure and feed back brain activity in real time. Our work spans OpenNFT software, closed-loop experiments, and research into rehabilitation applications.

Real-time fMRIOpenNFTRehabilitation
Discover our tools
03 / UNDERSTAND

Cognitive neuroscience

Studying the brain networks behind emotion regulation, social perception, and behavior. We use functional imaging and connectivity analysis to understand how these systems interact.

Emotion regulationConnectivityDCM
