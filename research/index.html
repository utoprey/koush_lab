Research

Multimodal AI

We investigate machine-learning approaches for integrating EEG and fMRI. By combining the temporal and spatial views of brain activity, we study cross-modal prediction, including the prediction of fMRI signals from EEG.

  • EEG–fMRI integration and cross-modal prediction
  • Deep learning, foundation models, and generative AI
  • Machine learning for real-time neurofeedback

Related publications · pyOpenNFT

Real-Time Neurofeedback

Our laboratory develops tools for monitoring brain activity and delivering feedback in real time. We work on closed-loop experimental systems, real-time fMRI processing and quality assessment, and research into neurofeedback applications in rehabilitation.

  • Real-time functional MRI and closed-loop experiments
  • OpenNFT and pyOpenNFT software
  • Neurofeedback in healthy and clinical populations

Open-source software · Related publications

Cognitive Neuroscience

We study neural mechanisms of emotion regulation, social perception, and behavior. Using functional imaging and connectivity analysis, we investigate how brain networks interact and how their dynamics relate to cognition and individual differences.

  • Positive social emotions and emotion regulation
  • Functional and effective connectivity
  • Dynamic causal modeling (DCM)
